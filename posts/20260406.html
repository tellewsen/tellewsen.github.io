Started boot.dev and Bandit

2026-04-06T15:55:00+02:00

Started doing some boot.dev and OverTheWire's Bandit game lately.

I paid for boot.dev. It's a bit expensive, and I don't really care about most of the gamification stuff, but the streak is kind of fun and does make me keep going. It's a good introduction to programming in general, and the intro to new languages is pretty good

Bandit is also fun. It's basically: ssh in, run some commands you have to look up, and get the password for the next level. It's a bit more terminal-focused than boot.dev, which is nice. I like that it's more hands-on and less tutorial-y.

Anyway, both are good. More terminal reps, less tutorial coma.
